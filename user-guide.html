Welcome to Homestay Manager! / Selamat Datang ke Homestay Manager!
An all-in-one, mobile-first management system for homestay hosts, apartment managers, and room rental operators.
Sistem pengurusan lengkap untuk pemilik homestay, apartmen, dan bilik sewa terus dari telefon pintar anda.

🗓️ Guide version / Versi panduan: for app v2.8.3 and later — updated / dikemas kini 16 Oct 2026

1. How to Install on Your Phone / Cara Pasang di Telefon Pintar

You do not need to download anything from the App Store or Google Play. The app installs directly from your web browser as a lightweight, fast app.

🍏 iPhone & iPad (iOS Safari)

  1. Open the app link provided to you in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square icon with an arrow pointing up at the bottom).
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen" (Tambah ke Skrin Utama).
  4. Tap Add in the top-right corner. The app icon will appear on your home screen!

🤖 Android (Google Chrome / Samsung Internet)

  1. Open the app link in Google Chrome.
  2. Tap the Three Dots Menu (⋮) at the top right.
  3. Tap "Install App" or "Add to Home screen" (Pasang Aplikasi).
  4. Confirm by tapping Install.

💡 Works Offline: Once added to your home screen, the app opens full-screen like a native app and works even without internet connection!

Common ways of typing it all work (0123456789, +60123456789, +60 12-345 6789). Do not add a 0 after +60.   6. Tap "Activate Full App".
  7. The header badge changes to ✨ PRO ACTIVE and the Pro Hub shows your renewal date. Done!

🇬🇧 English

Typing a long key by hand is the most common reason for activation problems, so please copy and paste it instead. 1. In WhatsApp, press and hold the message that contains only the key, then tap Copy. 2. Open the app and go to Enter License Key. 3. Tap the Paste button next to the key box. (Or tap inside the box, press and hold, and choose Paste.) 4. Check the line "Cleaned key:" under the box. It should show your key like STAY-XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX. 5. Check the line "We will use:" under the phone box, then tap Activate Full App.

Good to know: * You can even paste the whole WhatsApp message. The app finds the key inside it. * Extra spaces, long dashes (–), missing hyphens or small letters are fixed automatically. * A real key never contains 0, 1, I or O. If you see those characters, something was typed wrongly. * If the Paste button does nothing on your phone, use press and hold in the box and choose Paste. * Please do not retype the key. If it still fails, message us and we will issue a new one.

📲 C. Using Your License on More Than One Device / Guna Lesen di Lebih Daripada Satu Peranti

🇬🇧 English

  • One key works on up to 2 devices (for example your phone and a tablet), unless the seller told you a different limit.
  • On each device: install the app, then activate with the same key and the same WhatsApp number.
  • Switching to a new phone? Install the app, restore your data (see section 12), then activate again. If the app says the device limit is reached, message the seller at +60 12-402 2462 and ask to reset your device list, then activate again.
  • Your license is not part of your backup file. Restoring a backup brings back your homestays, bookings and settings, but you activate the license separately. This keeps your key safe if a backup file is shared.

🇲🇾 Bahasa Melayu

  • Satu kunci boleh digunakan pada sehingga 2 peranti (contohnya telefon dan tablet), kecuali penjual memberitahu had yang berbeza.
  • Pada setiap peranti: pasang aplikasi, kemudian aktifkan dengan kunci dan nombor WhatsApp yang sama.
  • Tukar telefon baru? Pasang aplikasi, pulihkan data anda (lihat bahagian 12), kemudian aktifkan semula. Jika aplikasi memaparkan had peranti dicapai, mesej penjual di +60 12-402 2462 dan minta senarai peranti anda ditetapkan semula, kemudian aktifkan semula.
  • Lesen anda tidak termasuk dalam fail sandaran. Memulihkan sandaran mengembalikan unit, tempahan dan tetapan anda, tetapi lesen diaktifkan secara berasingan. Ini melindungi kunci anda jika fail sandaran dikongsi.

🌐 D. Working Offline & Periodic Check-Ins / Bekerja Tanpa Internet & Semakan Berkala

🇬🇧 English

  • After activation the app works offline. Bookings, invoices, receipts and agreements do not need internet.
  • When you are online, the app quietly renews your license about once a week. You do not need to do anything.
  • If you stay offline for a long time (about 2 months), the Pro Hub will show Needs reconnect. Just open the app once with internet and it renews itself.
  • If it has been much longer, the app may ask you to enter your key again. Your data is safe; just activate again.
  • Keep your phone's date and time correct. If the clock is set earlier than before, the app will ask you to fix it.
  • Privacy: the license check sends only your key, your phone number and a random device ID. Your bookings and guest data never leave your phone.

🇲🇾 Bahasa Melayu

  • Selepas pengaktifan, aplikasi berfungsi tanpa internet. Tempahan, invois, resit dan perjanjian tidak memerlukan internet.
  • Apabila anda dalam talian, aplikasi memperbaharui lesen anda secara senyap kira-kira seminggu sekali. Anda tidak perlu buat apa-apa.
  • Jika anda tidak berhubung internet untuk tempoh yang lama (kira-kira 2 bulan), Hab Pro akan memaparkan Perlu sambung semula. Cuma buka aplikasi sekali dengan internet dan ia memperbaharui sendiri.
  • Jika sudah terlalu lama, aplikasi mungkin meminta anda memasukkan kunci semula. Data anda selamat; cuma aktifkan semula.
  • Pastikan tarikh dan masa telefon anda betul. Jika jam ditetapkan lebih awal daripada sebelumnya, aplikasi akan meminta anda membetulkannya.
  • Privasi: semakan lesen hanya menghantar kunci, nombor telefon dan ID peranti rawak anda. Data tempahan dan tetamu anda tidak pernah meninggalkan telefon anda.

    • 🏡 Entire House / Whole Unit (Whole House / Villa)

    • 🏢 Studio / Apartment Suite

5. Recording Bookings (Daily vs. Monthly) / Merekod Tempahan (Harian & Bulanan)

Tap the floating + (Add) button on the bottom-right of your screen to open the booking window.

☀️ A. Daily / Short-Term Stays (Holidays & Weekend Trips)

  1. Select "Daily / Short-Term" at the top. (Monthly fields are automatically blocked).
  2. Select your Homestay / Room Unit.
  3. Fill in guest particulars: Full Name, WhatsApp Phone, NRIC / Passport, Email, and Residential Address.
  4. Choose Check-In and Check-Out dates (nights are calculated automatically).
  5. Adjust nightly rate, cleaning fee, or refundable security deposit if needed.
  6. Use Quick Deposit Presets: Tap 30%, 50%, 100% Full, or 0 to instantly calculate deposit.
  7. Tap "Save Booking".

📅 B. Monthly Tenancy (Students, Working Adults, Corporate Leases / Sewaan Syarikat)

  1. Select "Monthly Tenancy" at the top. (Daily stay fields are automatically blocked).
  2. Select your Homestay / Room Unit.
  3. Fill in tenant particulars: Full Name, WhatsApp Phone, NRIC / Passport, Email, and Residential Address.
  4. Corporate Tenant Details (Optional / Pilihan Sewaan Syarikat): * When renting to a corporate entity, fill in:
    • Company Registration No (SSM): e.g. 202301048892 (1542806-X)
    • Authorized Contact Person (Pegawai Dihubungi): e.g. Mohd Taufik bin Ramli
    • Designation / Capacity (Jawatan): e.g. Managing Director / HR Manager
    • Contact Person NRIC (No. KP): e.g. 850315-10-5421
    • Contact Person Phone (No. Tel): e.g. +60198765432 (enables 1-tap direct messaging)
  5. Choose Tenancy Start Date and Duration (e.g. 6 Months).
  6. Enter: * Monthly Rent (e.g. RM 1,200) * Rental Deposit (e.g. RM 1,200) * Utilities Deposit (e.g. RM 300) * Tenancy Agreement & Stamping Fee (e.g. RM 150)
  7. The app automatically calculates the Initial Move-In Settlement Package.
  8. Tap "Save Booking".

💡 Calendar View & Smart Check-In / Check-Out Modes / Paparan Kalendar Pintar: * 📥 Check-In Mode: Highlights arrival dates with total check-in count badges (e.g. 📥 1). Days without arrivals remain clean. Allows hosts to immediately see daily arrivals. * 📤 Check-Out Mode & Vacating Units: Highlights guest departure dates (e.g. 📤 1). Clicking a check-out date displays dedicated Vacating Unit Cards with a 1-tap [+ Book This Unit Starting Today] button to instantly re-book the vacating unit for a new guest arriving today! * 🛏️ All Stays Mode: Shows all ongoing overnight stays with colored dots for each homestay property. * 4-Metric Date Summary Bar: Tapping any date instantly shows: [📥 Check-In: X] [📤 Check-Out: Y] [🛏️ In-Stay: Z] [✨ Available Tonight: W].

🏢 1. Guaranteed Particulars Printed Inside the Tenancy Agreement

Mukadimah (Preamble): States that the tenant is legally represented by the named Contact Person. 2. Jadual Pertama (First Schedule) Item 3: A dedicated highlighted box displaying the Contact Person's name, designation, NRIC, and mobile number. 3. Meterai & Tandatangan (Signatures): Dedicated signature block for the Authorized Representative to execute the agreement on behalf of the Tenant.

👤 2. Prominent Contact Person Section in Form Modal

Inside the 📝 Borang Perjanjian modal, the Pegawai Dihubungi / Wakil Penyewa (Contact Person & Authorized Representative) card is permanently visible and accessible. Whether you rent to a company or individual, you can easily view, enter, and edit these contact particulars with zero hassle!

7. Digital Payment Receipts & Bank Reference Capture / Rekod Resit Digital & No. Rujukan Bank

📸 1. Capturing Payment Receipts

12. Zero-Data-Loss Updates & Backups / Kemas Kini Tanpa Hilang Data

🔒 100% Data Preservation Guarantee (Why Your Data is Always Safe)

  • Technical Guarantee: All your homestay units, active bookings, guest particulars, turnover schedules, and income/expenses are stored in your device browser's persistent localStorage. Your license activation is stored securely on your device, separately from your business data.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) Regarding Updates

  • Q: Will updating the app delete my bookings or customer phone numbers?
    A: No, never. Your records are stored in persistent local storage on your phone, completely isolated from application code updates.
  • Q: Do I need to buy a new license or re-enter my license key after updating?
    A: No. Updates keep your Pro activation. You only need to enter your key again if you move to a new phone, clear the app's data, or the app asks you to reconnect after a very long time offline.
  • Q: Does my backup file contain my license?
    A: No, by design. This protects your key if a backup file is shared. Restore your data from the backup, then activate again.
  • Q: Does the app send my bookings or guest details to your server?
    A: No. The license check sends only your key, phone number and a random device ID. Your business data stays on your phone.
  • Q: What if I update while having no internet connection?
    A: The app continues functioning offline seamlessly with your existing version. As soon as you reconnect to the internet, it will detect the update banner.
  • Q: How do I transfer my homestay data when I upgrade to a new phone?
    A: Tap Export Backup (.json) on your old phone > Send the file to your new phone (via WhatsApp, AirDrop, or Email) > Open the app on your new phone > Tap Import Backup (.json). Everything restores in 2 seconds! Then tap Enter License Key and activate again (if the device limit is reached, message the seller to reset it).
