## 1. How to Install on Your Phone / Cara Pasang di Telefon Pintar

You do not need to download anything from the App Store or Google Play. The app installs directly from your web browser as a lightweight, fast app.

### 🍏 iPhone & iPad (iOS Safari)
1. Open the app link provided to you in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share button** (the square icon with an arrow pointing up at the bottom).
3. Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen"** (*Tambah ke Skrin Utama*).
4. Tap **Add** in the top-right corner. The app icon will appear on your home screen!

### 🤖 Android (Google Chrome / Samsung Internet)
1. Open the app link in **Google Chrome**.
2. Tap the **Three Dots Menu (⋮)** at the top right.
3. Tap **"Install App"** or **"Add to Home screen"** (*Pasang Aplikasi*).
4. Confirm by tapping **Install**.

> 💡 **Works Offline**: Once added to your home screen, the app opens full-screen like a native app and works even without internet connection!

## 2. Activating Your License Key / Cara Aktifkan Lesen Anda

1. Open the app and go to the **Settings (⚙️)** tab (or tap the **DEMO** badge in the header).
2. Tap **"Enter License Key"** (*Masukkan Kunci Lesen*).
3. Confirm your registered **WhatsApp Phone Number** (e.g. `+60123456789`).
4. Paste your **License Key** (e.g. `STAY-60123456789-XXXX-XXXX`).
5. Tap **"Activate Full App"**.
6. Your license is now active with unlimited homestays and zero recurring monthly fees!

## 3. Setting Up Your Profile & Bank / Tetapan Profil & Bank

To ensure your WhatsApp quotations and invoices display your correct business info:

1. Tap **Settings (⚙️)** in the bottom navigation.
2. Under **"Business & Localization"**:
   * **Homestay / Business Name**: e.g., *Bayu Homestay & Rooms*
   * **Currency**: Select `RM` (Ringgit Malaysia), `$`, etc.
   * **Owner Phone**: Your official WhatsApp contact number.
   * **Default Quotation Validity (Days)**: How long price quotes remain valid before expiring (e.g., 3 days).
   * **Standard WhatsApp Notes / Footer**: Default house rules, booking policy, or custom notes automatically appended at the bottom of customer WhatsApp messages.
   * **Language**: Choose `English` or `Bahasa Melayu` (You can also tap the `EN/BM` button at the top header anytime).
3. Under **"Banking & Payment Details"**:
   * Enter your **Bank Name** (e.g., *Maybank*, *CIMB*, *Public Bank*).
   * Enter your **Account Number**, **Account Holder Name**, and **DuitNow ID / Phone Number**.
4. Tap **"Save Preferences"** and **"Save Payment Details"**.

## 4. Adding Homestays & Room Rentals / Tambah Unit & Bilik Sewa

You can manage entire houses, villas, apartments, or individual room rentals:

1. Tap **Settings (⚙️)** > Tap **"+ Add Homestay Unit"** (or tap **"+ Add"** in the top header).
2. Fill in the unit details:
   * **Unit Name**: e.g., *Sunset Villa*, *Seaview Suite 3A*
   * **Room / Unit No**: e.g., *Master Bedroom*, *Room 2*, *Entire House*
   * **Rental Category**:
     * `🏡 Entire House / Whole Unit` (Whole House / Villa)
     * `🚪 Room Rental - Master Bedroom`
     * `🚪 Room Rental - Medium Room`
     * `🚪 Room Rental - Single Room`
     * `🏢 Studio / Apartment Suite`

   * **GPS Location / Google Maps Link**: Direct Google Maps / Waze link or coordinates (e.g. `https://maps.app.goo.gl/...` or `3.1390, 101.6869`) for instant guest navigation.

   * **WiFi SSID & Password**: Automatically included in guest guide messages.
   * **Standard Nightly Rate & Cleaning Fee**: Standard pricing for daily stays.
   * **Check-In & Check-Out Times**: e.g., 3:00 PM and 12:00 PM.
3. Tap **"Save Property"**.

## 5. Recording Bookings (Daily vs. Monthly) / Merekod Tempahan (Harian & Bulanan)

Tap the floating **`+` (Add)** button on the bottom-right of your screen to open the booking window.

### ☀️ A. Daily / Short-Term Stays (Holidays & Weekend Trips)
1. Select **"Daily / Short-Term"** at the top. *(Monthly fields are automatically blocked).*
2. Select your **Homestay / Room Unit**.
3. Fill in guest particulars: **Full Name**, **WhatsApp Phone**, **NRIC / Passport**, **Email**, and **Residential Address**.
4. Choose **Check-In** and **Check-Out** dates (nights are calculated automatically).
5. Adjust nightly rate, cleaning fee, or refundable security deposit if needed.
6. Use **Quick Deposit Presets**: Tap `30%`, `50%`, `100% Full`, or `0` to instantly calculate deposit.
7. Tap **"Save Booking"**.

---

### 📅 B. Monthly Tenancy (Students, Working Adults, Long Stays)
1. Select **"Monthly Tenancy"** at the top. *(Daily stay fields are automatically blocked).*
2. Select your **Homestay / Room Unit**.
3. Fill in tenant particulars: **Full Name**, **WhatsApp Phone**, **NRIC / Passport**, **Email**, and **Residential Address**.
4. Choose **Tenancy Start Date** and **Duration (e.g. 6 Months)**.
5. Enter:
   * **Monthly Rent** (e.g. RM 1,200)
   * **Rental Deposit** (e.g. RM 1,200)
   * **Utilities Deposit** (e.g. RM 300)
   * **Tenancy Agreement & Stamping Fee** (e.g. RM 150)
6. The app automatically calculates the **Initial Move-In Settlement Package**.
7. Tap **"Save Booking"**.

> 💡 **Calendar View & Smart Check-In / Check-Out Modes / Paparan Kalendar Pintar**:
> * **📥 Check-In Mode**: Highlights arrival dates with total check-in count badges (e.g. `📥 1`). Days without arrivals remain clean. Allows hosts to immediately see daily arrivals.
> * **📤 Check-Out Mode & Vacating Units**: Highlights guest departure dates (e.g. `📤 1`). Clicking a check-out date displays dedicated **Vacating Unit Cards** with a 1-tap `[+ Book This Unit Starting Today]` button to instantly re-book the vacating unit for a new guest arriving today!
> * **🛏️ All Stays Mode**: Shows all ongoing overnight stays with colored dots for each homestay property.
> * **4-Metric Date Summary Bar**: Tapping any date instantly shows: `[📥 Check-In: X] [📤 Check-Out: Y] [🛏️ In-Stay: Z] [✨ Available Tonight: W]`.

```
│  Month 1 (01 Sep - 30 Sep) • RM 1,200  [ 🟢 PAID (Move-In) ] │

│  Month 3 (01 Nov - 30 Nov) • RM 1,200  [ 🟡 PENDING ]        │

```

1. Go to the **Bookings** tab.
2. On any monthly tenancy card, tap the **`📑 Monthly Invoices (X/Y)`** button.

   * **To Send an Invoice**: Tap **`Invoice`** next to that month. The WhatsApp window opens with the billing period, due date, rent breakdown, and bank info. You can also type any **Utility / Extra Surcharges** (e.g. TNB electricity arrears) before sending!

   * **To Send a Payment Receipt**: Tap **`Receipt`** to generate an official WhatsApp payment receipt (`REC-RENT-M2-XXXX`).

## 7. Digital Payment Receipts & Bank Reference Capture / Rekod Resit Digital & No. Rujukan Bank

When guests or tenants transfer booking deposits, balance settlements, or monthly rentals via online banking (DuitNow QR, Maybank2u, CIMB, TNG eWallet, etc.) and forward their receipt slips in WhatsApp:

### 📸 1. Capturing Payment Receipts
* On any booking card, tap **`+ Resit / + Receipt`**, or tap **`Mark Deposit Paid`** / **`Mark Fully Paid`**.
* The **Capture Payment Receipt & Reference** modal opens with the recommended amount pre-filled.
* Select the payment purpose: `🟡 Booking Deposit`, `🟢 Remaining Balance`, `🟢 Full Payment`, `📑 Monthly Rental`, or `🔒 Security Deposit`.

### 🏦 2. Quick Bank Selector & Paste Reference ID
* Tap any quick bank pill: `Maybank`, `CIMB`, `Bank Islam`, `Public Bank`, `RHB`, `Hong Leong`, `DuitNow QR`, `Touch n Go`, `Cash`, or `Other`.
* Tap **`Paste`** next to *Bank Ref No. / Transaction ID* to instantly paste the tenant's transaction reference number.

### ⚡ 3. Automatic 97% Image Compression
* Tap or drag the tenant's screenshot slip into the upload dropzone.
* The built-in image processor automatically downscales camera photos from 3MB–8MB down to **~40KB–70KB (97% compression)** while keeping account numbers, reference IDs, and timestamps completely sharp.
* This protects your device storage and ensures fast, lag-free loading.

### 🧾 4. Receipt Gallery & Full-Screen Lightbox
* Tap the **`🧾 X Receipt(s)`** badge on any booking card to open the **Receipt Gallery**.
* View total paid vs remaining balance with an interactive financial progress bar.
* Tap **`Copy`** to copy the transaction reference number with 1 tap.
* Tap **`View Slip`** to open the full-screen Lightbox viewer, or tap **`Download Slip`** to save the slip to your device.

### 💬 5. Automatic Bank Reference in WhatsApp Receipts
* Whenever you generate an official WhatsApp receipt (`Deposit Receipt`, `Full Receipt & Keys`, or `Monthly Rent Receipt`), the app automatically inserts the tenant's bank reference number:
  > `• 🔖 No. Rujukan Bank: M2U-99218291 (Maybank)`

## 8. 1-Tap WhatsApp Automation / Automasi Mesej WhatsApp

## 12. Zero-Data-Loss Updates & Backups / Kemas Kini Tanpa Hilang Data

### 🔒 100% Data Preservation Guarantee (Why Your Data is Always Safe)
* **Technical Guarantee**: All your homestay units, active bookings, guest particulars, turnover schedules, income/expenses, and license keys are stored in your device browser's persistent **`localStorage`**.
* **Code vs Data Separation**: When an update is deployed, only the application code (HTML, CSS, JavaScript) stored in the Service Worker Cache is updated. **Your `localStorage` database is completely separate and is never deleted, wiped, or modified during an update.**
* **Offline Resilient**: You can safely update even on mobile data without worrying about losing past records.

---

### 🔄 How End Users Update the App (4 Easy Methods)

#### Method 1: Automatic 1-Tap Update Notification Banner (Recommended)
1. Whenever a new version is released, the app automatically detects it in the background.
2. A prominent notification banner will appear at the top of the app:
   > 🚀 **App Update Available! (vX.X.X)** — *A new version with enhancements is ready.*  
   > `[Update Now]` `[Dismiss]`
3. Tap **"Update Now"** (or *Kemas Kini Sekarang*).
4. The app refreshes instantly (~1 second) with all the latest features, while 100% of your homestay units, bookings, and settings remain untouched.

#### Method 2: Manual "Check for Updates" via Settings
1. Tap **Settings (⚙️)** in the bottom navigation.
2. Scroll to the **"App Version & Updates"** section.
3. Tap **"🔍 Check for Updates"** (*Semak Kemas Kini*).
4. If an update is available, the app activates the new version immediately. If you are already up to date, a message confirms: *"You are using the latest version!"*.

#### Method 3: Mobile Home Screen App (PWA) — Close & Reopen
1. If you installed the app on your iPhone or Android home screen:
2. Simply swipe the app away from your recent apps list (close it completely).
3. Reopen the app while connected to the internet.
4. The background Service Worker automatically downloads the updated files in the background and activates them on your next launch.

#### Method 4: 1-Tap Safety Backup & Restore (Zero-Risk Guarantee)
Although updates are completely non-destructive, taking regular backups is good practice for every business owner:
1. Go to **Settings (⚙️)** > Scroll down to **"Data Backup & Export"**.
2. Tap **"Export Backup (.json)"** (*Eksport Sandaran*).
3. A `.json` file containing your entire business data (all homestays, bookings, expenses, turnovers, and license) will be saved to your device, iCloud, Google Drive, or WhatsApp.
4. **To Restore**: If you ever switch to a brand-new phone or tablet, simply open the app, tap **"Import Backup (.json)"**, select your file, and all your records are restored instantly!

### ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) Regarding Updates

* **Q: Will updating the app delete my bookings or customer phone numbers?**  
  **A:** **No, never.** Your records are stored in persistent local storage on your phone, completely isolated from application code updates.
* **Q: Do I need to buy a new license or re-enter my license key after updating?**  
  **A:** **No.** Your license key is permanently stored and validated locally. Updates preserve your Pro / Master activation status automatically.
* **Q: What if I update while having no internet connection?**  
  **A:** The app continues functioning offline seamlessly with your existing version. As soon as you reconnect to the internet, it will detect the update banner.
* **Q: How do I transfer my homestay data when I upgrade to a new phone?**  
  **A:** Tap **Export Backup (.json)** on your old phone > Send the file to your new phone (via WhatsApp, AirDrop, or Email) > Open the app on your new phone > Tap **Import Backup (.json)**. Everything restores in 2 seconds!
